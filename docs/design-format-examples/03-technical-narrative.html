Direction A / implementation design

A8: stabilising full-rules Phase 1

Status: scale hypothesis confirmed; completion gate partial · 13 July 2026

Purpose

Move the validated A5 self-play recipe from Tiny-Heat to full generated tracks while preserving honest partial observability, seat-count randomisation, and a disjoint evaluation contract.

Design decision. Keep the masked head, flat encoder, snapshot-pool recipe, and sparse placement reward. Treat the completed 1M campaign as the control for one-factor stability experiments.

Contract

training namespace: 80008
seat schedule:       balanced shuffle of [2, 3, 4, 5, 6]
track distribution:  length 50–90, 3–7 corners, 2 laps
policy:              shared masked PPO
promotion evidence:  validation band only
final claim:         untouched test band

Evidence

The scaled campaign produced more than 4,000 completed games per seed. Aggregate mean placement reward was positive in every evaluated seat/opponent cell. Four- and six-seat win rates cleared their chance baselines against the weak field; the two-seat result remained seed-sensitive.

Implementation sequence

  1. Introduce separate validation and final-test namespaces.
  2. Encode a pre-declared checkpoint-selection rule in the report path.
  3. Run the unchanged 1M configuration as the reproducible control.
  4. Run one stabilisation arm at equal seeds and budget.
  5. Open the final-test band only after the decision rule is frozen.

Acceptance

  • Same seed reproduces the same checkpoint and report.
  • No checkpoint is selected independently per seat or opponent cell.
  • All-seat aggregate placement reward remains positive across seeds.
  • The chosen policy clears the untouched final test without retuning.

Why this format

Numbered prose keeps implementation intent and rationale together. The margin holds durable constraints without interrupting the main reading flow.
