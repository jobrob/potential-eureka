The decision

The original 50k pilot was too small to answer the method question. At roughly one million nominal transitions, three seeds produced useful held-out play—especially at four and six seats—but no one checkpoint is dependable across the whole grid.

Recommended next moveSplit evaluation into a development validation band and a fresh untouched test band. Compare one stabilisation change against the unchanged 1M baseline before adding targets or architecture.

Completion criteria

A8 is complete when a pre-declared checkpoint rule produces stable, positive held-out placement reward across seeds and seat counts, and the chosen policy survives a fresh test band that was not used for iteration.

  • Same seed reproduces the same checkpoint and report.
  • No per-seat or per-opponent checkpoint cherry-picking.
  • Every seat-count aggregate remains useful across seeds.
  • The final claim survives the untouched test without retuning.
