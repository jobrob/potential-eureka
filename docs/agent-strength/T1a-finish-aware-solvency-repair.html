T1a · Repair target

The projection wraps around before asking whether the car has finished.

project_solvency walks to the next corner using modulo track coordinates. On the final straight, that corner belongs to a lap the player will never drive. Its projected heat cost suppresses faster gears in 408 decisions across all 60 T1 losses.

01 · Small implementation

Compare absolute race progress with absolute finish progress.

PartFrozen design
RepairIn project_solvency, track lap-aware absolute progress. Before charging a projected corner, stop when that corner starts at or beyond (track.laps + 1) × track.length.
PreservationCorners before the finish retain the existing cooldown, overage, shortfall, and penalty math. Multi-corner horizons advance absolute progress past each inspected corner.
Focused testsA next-lap corner contributes zero on the final straight; the same corner remains priced on an earlier lap; and the known final-straight planner state now agrees with the no-future-corner counterfactual.
