L01 · Scope

This stream studies policies produced by training.

Its question is not whether the heuristic or search bot can be hand-improved. It asks why trained policies reach their present level, whether that level rises with scale, and which part of the learning process limits breadth and stability.

Implementation deferredNo new encoder, action head, reward, auxiliary target, league, optimizer, averaging method, or training campaign is selected here. Investigations must first distinguish scale limitation from algorithmic or representational limitation.

L04 · Blocking unknowns

Several incompatible explanations still fit the same scores.

  • Scale.
    Does the same recipe improve at 3M, 10M, and later milestones, or cycle faster?
  • Local decisions.
    Are losses driven by repeatable tactical errors or noisy long-horizon outcomes?
  • Self-play population.
    Does current/snapshot play forget styles or converge to exploitable conventions?
  • Representation.
    Does the flat/masked policy underuse private hand, traffic, opponent, or track information?
  • Learning signal.
    Is terminal placement too delayed or noisy for the hardest decisions?
  • Selection.
    Can checkpoint choice or combination recover skill reliably without hiding recipe instability?
  • Absolute standard.
    How far is G0002 from competent humans or a trusted stronger oracle?
