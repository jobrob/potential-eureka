T01 · Scope

This stream studies agents whose behavior is fixed by code and configuration.

Its goal is to understand and improve scripted reasoning, state evaluation, determinization, and search while preserving reproducibility and practical latency. It does not tune PPO, change learned representations, or judge a static change solely by whether G0002 finds it harder to beat.

T2a implementation adoptedThe first evidence-backed repair is now versioned as the V2 static population. Search depth, determinization count, leaf evaluator, and rollout policy remain unchanged; wider tuning is still deferred.

T04 · Blocking unknowns

We do not yet know whether the main defect is strategy, evaluation, or approximation.

  • Weak-field paradox.
    Which decisions let StaticSearchV1 beat repaired play yet lose to the weaker heuristic style?
  • Leaf quality.
    Does progress evaluation reward intended movement, unsafe traffic lines, or short-term gains that exact future play reverses?
  • Hidden-information handling.
    Are two determinizations enough, biased, or irrelevant compared with evaluator error?
  • Heuristic failure distribution.
    After the spin fix, which recurring errors dominate: gear, cards, traffic, slipstream, heat, or finish timing?
  • Track and seat regimes.
    Do failures concentrate in limit-1 corners, clustered corners, multi-lane tracks, pack traffic, or large fields?
  • Search frontier.
    What is the best strength attainable at 1, 5, 10, 25, and 100 ms per decision?
  • Exploitability.
    Are apparent improvements broad or narrow counters to one fixed opponent?
  • Reference quality.
    Without humans or a trusted oracle, when is a static bot genuinely better rather than merely different?

T07 · Boundary

T2a converted the repeated defects into a faster V2 population.

Status: T0, T1, T1a, T2, and T2a complete.
The finish-aware repair remains adopted. T2a implemented round/hand-aware cache identity, safety-aware search admission, and avoidable Stress-risk guards. Against unchanged V1 fields, weak V2 used 0.5% fewer rounds with essentially flat reward, repaired V2 used 5.7% fewer rounds with +0.006 reward, and StaticSearchV2 used 12.8% fewer rounds with +0.217 reward. A paired timing replay reduced search planning time by 23.8% and milliseconds per decision by 4.2%. V1 remains frozen for historical replay; V2 is the recommended development static population.
