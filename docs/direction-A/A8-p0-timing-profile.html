test_a8_phase1.pyOne focused test checks timing keys are present, finite, non-negative, and internally consistent.

The profiler is opt-in. With profiling disabled the collector skips all per-decision clock calls, preserving the baseline training path.

03 · Accounting model

Nested measurements become exclusive report categories.

rollout = simulation + encoding + inference
timed training = rollout + preparation + PPO update + overhead

simulation = max(0, rollout - encoding - inference)

rollout is the primary decision metric. The inner split tells us whether a future collector should focus on rules-engine concurrency, observation construction, or batched neural inference. Preparation is reported separately so GAE and tensor concatenation cannot silently inflate either side.
