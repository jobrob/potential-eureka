02 · Replay support

The project had replay ingredients, not a diagnostic replay.

The engine already had deterministic game seeds, an event log, a human formatter, and internal action replay for MCTS. heat.engine.events still described replay serialization as future work, and the event log did not preserve agent decision alternatives. Nothing useful had been removed from the current history; the general serialized replay anticipated by the module was simply never implemented.

H1 adds the smallest missing layer in experiments/diag_h1_heuristic.py. Every race is addressed by four primitives—seat count, track seed, game seed, and strong seat. Selected losses are rerun from those coordinates. A wrapper records the strong action, a weak-agent shadow action from the same public state, and enough context to classify the decision. The replay aborts if its finish order differs from the screen.

05 · Decisive local diagnosis

Limit-1 corners can trap the planner in repeated certain spins.

Thirteen replayed losses contain a strong-agent spin, spanning 2, 4, and 6 seats. They contain 202 spin events in total: 66 at two seats, 89 at four, and 47 at six. Five races spin 11–88 times. This is not ordinary risk variance; it is a recovery failure that stretches races to 48–140 rounds.

06 · Implemented follow-up

A safety guard was tested before touching weights or multiplayer strategy.

  1. Detect
    plays that guarantee a spin even at minimum Stress speed
  2. Filter
    only when a legal non-spinning play exists
  3. Replay
    the five repeated-spin coordinates
  4. Gate
    fresh development games at 2, 4, and 6 seats

The correction should be a planner safety invariant, not a global increase to the spin penalty. A weight change can create new conservatism and still leaves certain spins available as an argmax. The focused gate should require zero repeated-spin locks, no increase in total spins, identical legality and determinism, and no material loss in the unchanged 108-game screen.

The initial shortcut—treating evaluator p_spinout == 1 as certainty—was too broad because Stress can resolve to a lower speed. The implemented guard instead evaluates the lowest possible speed from the available Basic cards, preserving legal Stress recovery choices.

08 · Limits

The repair is proven locally; broader heuristic quality remains open.

  • Loss-only sample.
    The classifier was applied to fixed losses, not a matched set of wins. Frequency identifies recurring failure candidates, not causal effect size.
  • Shadow choices are contrasts.
    The weak agent's action from the same public state does not replay the full race under that alternative.
  • Signals overlap.
    Congestion, a declined slipstream, lower gear, and a spin can occur in one race. Counts must not be added as independent explanations.
  • Development data only.
    The 700000+ band is now heuristic-development data. It must not become the A8 untouched ML final test.
  • No broad rewrite justified.
    Traffic prediction and slipstream valuation remain possible later investigations, but H1 did not isolate a comparably small, causal correction for them.
