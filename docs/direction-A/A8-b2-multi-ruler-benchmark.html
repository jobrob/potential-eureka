06 · Reliability and limits

The matchup estimates are useful; the recipe-frequency claim remains modest.

  • Within-cell precision.
    Two hundred races give an approximate 95% Wilson half-width of seven percentage points near a 50% win rate. Raw wins, rates, placement rewards, and Wilson bounds are retained for every cell.
  • Matched comparison.
    All contenders saw identical tracks and cell seeds, reducing evaluation noise in G0003−G0002 differences.
  • Independent training evidence.
    There are only three runs per generation. Two improve and one regresses, so confidence in “G0003 usually helps” is materially weaker than confidence in these six checkpoints' matchup measurements.
  • Consumed track band.
    The 40-track 714000+ band is now benchmark-development data, not an untouched final test. A future final claim needs disjoint tracks.
  • Equal weighting is a policy choice.
    Each ruler × seat cell contributes equally. The report therefore preserves per-ruler and per-seat slices rather than presenting the aggregate as an objective law.
  • No universal ruler.
    B1's non-transitivity remains. “Positive against all suite medians” is broader evidence, not proof of optimal or human-level play.
